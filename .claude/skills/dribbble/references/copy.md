# Shot copy

Search accounts for over 80% of what people see on Dribbble, and it reads
title, description and tags. The image wins the click; the words decide whether
there is anything to click.

## Title

Formula: **`<Name or subject>: <deliverable> for <who or what>`**

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Exploration 03 | Nordlicht Coffee: brand identity for a Hamburg roastery |
| Logo 🔥 | Kanzlei Weber: monogram logo for a tax law firm |
| Branding | Fieldnote: visual system for a field-recording label |
| App UI | Ruhe: meditation app onboarding, dark mode |

- 40–70 characters. Front-load the words people search for ("brand identity",
  "logo", "packaging"), because the end gets cut in some views.
- A series shares the name and changes the deliverable:
  `Nordlicht Coffee: logo`, `… : colour and type system`, `… : packaging`.
- Concept or unsolicited work says so (`concept`, `rebrand concept`). It
  protects the user's credibility and is what people search for anyway.

## Description

40–120 words, four parts, plain sentences:

1. **Context**: who the client is and what they needed. One sentence.
2. **Idea**: the thought behind the work. The sentence that makes this shot
   different from the next one in the feed.
3. **What you see**: what this specific shot shows, and what the rest of the
   series shows.
4. **Invitation**: one line. Availability, a link to the full case, or a
   question. Not "Press L to like".

Example:

> Nordlicht is a small-batch roastery in Hamburg's harbour district. They wanted
> to look like the harbour, not like a third-wave café.
>
> The mark is built from a single tide line that turns into an N. It works
> as a stamp on bags and as a pattern at large scale.
>
> This shot shows the primary logo on the core navy. Colour, type and packaging
> follow in the next shots.
>
> Full case on hierholzer.design. Open for identity projects from January.

Credit collaborators and the client (with permission). No hashtags, no emoji
walls, no keyword lists in the description.

## Tags (max. 12)

Fill from specific to broad, and only with what is in the image:

1. **Deliverable** (2–3): `logo`, `brand identity`, `packaging`, `logo animation`
2. **Discipline** (1–2): `branding`, `visual identity`, `typography`
3. **Industry** (1–2): `coffee`, `law firm`, `music label`
4. **Form or style** (2–3): `monogram`, `wordmark`, `minimal`, `geometric`, `editorial`
5. **Medium** (0–2): `motion`, `3d`, `illustration`
6. **Place or language** (0–1): only if it helps, such as `hamburg` or `germany`
   for local clients

Use the common English term, not your own coinage (`brand identity`, not
`brand world`). Do not reuse an identical set across a series: vary the
deliverable tags per shot. Tag stuffing (irrelevant popular tags) gets shots
filtered out of categories.

## Output format

Give the user one paste-ready block per shot:

```
SHOT 1 · mark.png
Title:  Nordlicht Coffee: brand identity for a Hamburg roastery
Tags:   brand identity, logo, branding, coffee, roastery, monogram, minimal, packaging, hamburg
Description:
<text>
```

## Posting rhythm

- 1–2 shots a week, the same weekday. A series of five then covers a month.
- Post the strongest thumbnail first: it sets the first impression of the
  series and links visitors to the rest.
- The long case-study shot goes last, when the series has made people curious.
- Pin the best shot of the month to the profile.
